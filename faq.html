FAQ

Straight answers

Everything most people ask before booking. If something is not covered, just message us.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave a key or use a lockbox / smart lock. We will send a short update when we are finished and note anything that needs your attention.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. We bring all cleaning products and tools. We use effective, low-fragrance, pet- and kid-friendly formulas. If you prefer us to use your products, just let us know when booking.

What if I need to reschedule or cancel?

Just give us at least 24 hours notice and we will move your appointment at no charge. Last-minute cancellations may incur a small fee to cover the team time.

Are you insured?

Yes. We carry full liability insurance and every cleaner is background-checked. We are happy to provide proof of insurance on request.

What areas do you serve?

We currently cover the greater metro area and surrounding suburbs within about a 25-mile radius. If you are unsure whether we reach your postcode, just ask when you request a quote.

How long does a typical clean take?

A regular clean for a typical 2 to 3 bedroom home usually takes 2.5 to 4 hours. Deep cleans and move-outs take longer. We will give you a realistic time window with your quote.

What if I am not happy with something?

Tell us within 24 hours. We will come back and make it right at no extra cost. We would rather fix it than leave you frustrated.

How should I prepare for the first visit?

A light tidy of personal items helps us focus on cleaning rather than organizing. Clear kitchen counters and bathroom surfaces if you can — we will handle the rest.

Do you offer one-off cleans or only recurring?

Both. Many people start with a deep clean and then decide whether to set up a regular schedule. There is no obligation to continue.
